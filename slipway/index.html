Before you start

Do I need my own wheel? −

No. Lessons 1–4 are hand-building. From lesson 5 we lend a tabletop wheel for £15 a month, delivered with 10 kg of clay.

Where do my pots get fired? +

Post them in our padded box to the Stoke studio. We bisque and glaze-fire every Friday and send them back within ten days.

What if I miss a live session? +

Every session is recorded and broken into steps, so you can scrub straight to the bit you need.

Is clay messy at home? +

Less than you think. The kit has a splash pan, a slop bucket and a guide to setting up on a kitchen table.
