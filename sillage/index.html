The blending room

Two and a half hours upstairs with our perfumer, Ines. You smell the raw materials, build an accord on blotter strips, and leave with 30 ml of your own scent. We keep your formula on file, so you can reorder it.

  1. iSmell 24 raw materials, from bergamot to labdanum, and choose the six you like best.
  2. iiWeigh them drop by drop on the bench scales. We'll help with the ratios.
  3. iiiBottle and name it. It needs two weeks to settle, so we post it to you.

How long does a bottle last?

About eight months at three sprays a day. Kept out of the sun, an unopened bottle is good for five years.

Are the scents vegan?

Yes. We use synthetic musks and ambrette seed instead of animal materials, and nothing is tested on animals.

Can I return a bottle?

Unopened bottles within 30 days. If you've opened one and it isn't right, bring it in and we'll help you swap it.

Do you make wedding scents?

Yes. Book two blending-room sessions and we'll make a private formula with a run of 20 to 200 bottles.
