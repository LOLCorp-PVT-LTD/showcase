Questions

Before you send it.

Q.1Is 48 hours really 48 hours?

Two working days from when we confirm the fee. If we miss it, the review is free. It has happened twice, and both times we said so.

Q.2What if the contract is longer than 20 pages?

We'll quote a fixed fee on the first call. Most longer contracts are 20 pages that matter and 40 pages of schedules.

Q.3Will you talk to the other side for us?

If you want us to. Most clients prefer to send our summary themselves; it keeps the relationship friendly and costs nothing extra.

Q.4Do you only work in Leeds?

We're based in Leeds and work for businesses anywhere in England and Wales. About a third of our clients have never been to the office.

Q.5Are you regulated?

Yes. We are a firm of solicitors regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, with professional indemnity insurance for all the work we do.
