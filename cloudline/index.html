A house held off the cliff.

Two concrete floors cantilever six metres past the rock, with glass on three sides. Below the terrace, the valley fills with cloud most autumn mornings.

  1. The approach

    Lit limestone steps from the parking terrace, cut into the slope so the house appears only at the last turn.

  2. The cloud room

    The main bedroom upstairs: a king bed facing east, blackout linen and a balcony that runs the length of the glass.

  3. The long room

    Kitchen, a ten-seat oak table and a sunken sofa under one ceiling. The wood stove heats both floors in January.

  4. The ledge

    140 m² of terrace over a 300 m drop, with a glass edge, two loungers and the best sunrise in Val Varaita.

  5. The stair

    Warm light under every tread, timed to come on twenty minutes before sunset.

Every month is a different house.

Getting here.

  1. Turin airport · hire a car, 1 h 35 by the A6 and the valley road
  2. Sampeyre · last shop and fuel; we leave bread and milk in the house
  3. Borgata Colle · 4 km of gravel road, fine in any car from March to November
  4. Cloudline · park on the upper terrace and follow the lit stair down
Is the drive safe in winter?

From December to February we collect you from Sampeyre in a 4×4 whenever there's snow on the road. Chains are in the house if you'd rather drive.

When do the cloud seas happen?

On still, clear nights after rain. October and November give the most; we text guests the evening before when it looks likely.

Can we bring a dog?

One well-behaved dog is welcome for €25 a stay. The terrace edge is glass, so it's safe for dogs and children.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Fibre to the house at 300 Mbps, and a desk in the long room facing the window. Phones get one bar on the terrace.
