What we look at

Forty signals. One score.

Rules catch the fraud you saw last year. Tripwire scores the shape of the money: how it moves between accounts, not just how big each payment is.

01 · NETWORK

Mule hops

Money that lands and leaves within minutes, two or three accounts deep.

02 · DEVICE

New phone, old member

A first-time device, a fresh SIM, or an app installed ten minutes before a big payment.

03 · VELOCITY

Sudden speed

A member who pays twice a month suddenly pays six new people in an hour.

04 · PAYEE

Name does not match

Confirmation of Payee says close match or no match, and the member presses on anyway.

05 · BEHAVIOUR

On a call while paying

Long hesitation, copy-pasted account numbers and an active phone call: the classic scam script.

06 · ACCOUNT AGE

Opened last week

New receiving accounts with no salary, no bills and a sudden lump sum are where mules live.

  Questions
  
Things risk teams ask us.

  

    
Will it hold up genuine payments?
Rarely. At Severn Vale 0.3% of good payments got a check, and most of those were a single in-app question the member answered in seconds.

    
Do we need a data science team?
No. We train and monitor the model for you. Your team sets thresholds in plain language and sees every reason behind a score.

    
Where is our members' data kept?
Only in the UK, in two data centres we run in London and Manchester. Nothing is used to train models for other customers unless you opt in to shared mule intelligence.

    
Can it freeze accounts on its own?
Only if you let it. Most societies start with automatic holds on payments and human review for freezes, then automate the clearest cases.

    
How long is the contract?
Twelve months, with a free six-week shadow period first. If it doesn't catch anything you would have missed, you walk away.
