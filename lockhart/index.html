How it works

Sixty minutes, one way out.

1

Arrive 15 minutes early

Lockers for coats and phones, a pot of tea, and the briefing from your game master.

2

The door closes

It isn't really locked: there's a release on the inside and a camera watching for trouble.

3

Ask for hints

Three free hints, delivered by the lighthouse radio, the train guard or the vault telephone.

4

Get out (or don't)

Photo at the door either way, and your time on the board if you make it.

How old do you have to be?

Twelve and over, with an adult for under 16s. The Cartographer's Study works for 10 and up with a grown-up.

Is it scary?

No jump scares in any room. The Keeper's Last Night is dark and stormy; the others are well lit.

What if we get stuck?

Three free hints, and your game master will nudge you if you've been stuck too long. Nobody leaves without seeing the ending.
