Since 1962

Three dentists, one front door.

Dr Arthur Marsh opened the surgery in the front room of No. 14 the year the bus first came through Millbrook. His granddaughter Eleanor runs it now, in the same building, with a new chair, a quieter drill and the same green door.

We look after about 2,400 people from Millbrook, Upper Combe and the farms in between. Plenty of them have been coming since they were small enough to sit on a parent's knee in the chair.

“If you're nervous, tell us. We'll go at your pace.” — Dr Eleanor Marsh

Your visit

A check-up, minute by minute.

08:55Tuesday morning
  1. 08:55Through the green door

    Sue on reception says hello by name. There's tea, today's paper and a box of toys under the window.

  2. 09:00Into the chair

    We ask what's changed since last time and how your teeth feel. You can hold the mirror if you'd like to watch.

  3. 09:04The check, tooth by tooth

    Dr Marsh counts all 32, checks each one and your gums, and talks you through anything she finds.

  4. 09:14Hygienist: scale and polish

    Gentle scaling, then a polish. Raise your hand any time and we stop straight away.

  5. 09:28Out with a plan

    A printed plan, your next date in the diary and a sticker, whatever your age.

Children

Two minutes, four corners.

Children's check-ups are free on the NHS, and we like to see them from their first tooth. Practise at home with our brushing timer: thirty seconds for each corner of the mouth.

Questions

Asked at the desk.

I have toothache and it's the weekend.

Ring 01452 700 331 and the message gives the on-call number for Saturday and Sunday. Outside our hours you can also call NHS 111.

Can I bring my baby to my appointment?

Yes. Sue is very good at holding babies, and there's a changing mat in the downstairs loo.

Is there parking?

Two spaces behind the practice for patients, reached from Church Lane, and free parking on the green after 9.30.

Is the building accessible?

Surgery 1 is on the ground floor with level access from the garden gate. Tell us when you book and we'll meet you there.

How do I pay?

Card or contactless at the desk. Millbrook Plan members pay by monthly direct debit.
