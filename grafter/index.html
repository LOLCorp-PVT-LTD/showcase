Questions

Straight answers.

Is it really free for tradespeople?

Yes. No fees, no cut of your day rate, no subscription. Firms pay us when they book someone.

How do you check tickets?

We check every card and certificate with the scheme that issued it before it shows as verified, and again before it expires.

When do I get paid?

The firm signs off your hours by Wednesday and the money lands in your bank on Friday. If they don't sign off, we chase them, not you.

Can I work as a sole trader or through my company?

Either. Self-employed, limited company or CIS: tell us once and every invoice is set up properly.

Where do you cover?

All of Yorkshire, the North East and the North West today, with the Midlands from spring 2027.
