Switches

How it feels is a graph.

We lube every switch by hand and test a sample from each bag on a force gauge. These are the real curves: force against travel, down and back up.

K

Press it. A spring, a stem and 4 mm of travel. The cap you're pressing moves on the same spring model as the real switch: a little overshoot on the way back, then still.

Questions

Before you reserve.

Does it work with a Mac?

Yes, and with Windows and Linux. There's a switch on the back to swap the modifier keys, and you can remap anything in the browser.

Can I change the switches later?

Our boards are soldered for a quieter, more solid feel. We'll swap switches for you at the workshop, or send a hot-swap PCB for £45.

When is the deposit taken?

When you reserve. The rest is taken when your board ships, and the deposit is refundable until your case is milled.

Do you ship outside the UK?

To the EU, the US and Canada. Duties are included in the price at checkout, so there's nothing to pay on delivery.

What if something breaks?

Ten years on the case and two on the electronics. In practice, we repair anything we made, for as long as we're here.
