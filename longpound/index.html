Hold a boat

Narrowboat hire · Bradford-on-Avon wharf

No licence. No experience. We'll teach you the locks.

  1. 01Bring her in

    Steer into the empty chamber at walking pace and step off with the centre line. Our boats are 7 ft wide; the lock is 14, so two can share.

  2. 02Close the bottom gates

    Lean on the balance beam and walk it round. The gates seal themselves once water pushes from above.

  3. 03Wind up the top paddles

    Fit the windlass and give it about ten turns. Water pours in fast at first, then slows as the levels close.

  4. 04Open the top gates

    When the levels match, the gates swing easily. If they won't move, the water isn't level yet.

  5. 05Cruise out at 4 mph

    Close the gates and drop the paddles behind you. That was a 2.4 m rise, and it took about eight minutes.

How far will you get?

We plan on 3 mph, 15 min a lock and about 4½ hours at the tiller a day.

Prices · 2027 season

Hold a boat for a week, free.

Short breaks start on a Friday, midweek breaks on a Monday, weeks on either. We hold your dates for seven days while you sort out the crew.

Do I need a licence?

No. Your hire includes the canal licence and an hour's handover on the water, including your first lock with one of us.

Can we bring the dog?

Up to two dogs for £45 a trip. There's a towel by the door for muddy towpaths.

How hard are the locks?

A windlass, a bit of shoulder and about ten minutes each. The Caen Hill flight is 29 locks in two miles: plan a whole day.

Where do we park?

Free parking at the wharf for the length of your hire, two minutes from Bradford-on-Avon station.
