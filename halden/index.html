One acre of granite headland.

The house

Four bedrooms, one long room, and nothing between you and the horizon.

Designed by Marrow & Vale Architects, Halden House is arranged as two bars. Below is a glass living floor that opens onto a 14-metre pool terrace. Above is a timber-clad sleeping floor whose bedrooms each frame a different part of the coast.

Ground-source heat, a 22 kWp roof array and a 40 m³ rainwater cistern mean the house runs on the site it stands on.
