Since 1987

Type you can feel with your thumb.

Quoin Press started in a lean-to behind the cheese shop with one hand press and four cases of type. Thirty-nine years on we're at No. 31 Catherine Hill, with three presses, 214 founts of wood and metal type, and a window you can watch us through.

Letterpress pushes the ink into the paper, so every letter leaves a shallow bite you can run a finger across. Nothing digital looks like it.

How a job runs

Ten working days, door to door.

Day 1

We talk

Wording, quantities, paper and ink. Bring examples you love.

Day 2–3

We set and proof

Type set by hand, a proof pulled on Vandy, photographed and emailed.

Day 4

You approve

Changes are free until you say yes. After that, the type is locked up.

Day 5–8

We print

One colour per pass, drying overnight between colours.

Day 9–10

Trim and post

Guillotined, edge-painted if ordered, wrapped in glassine and sent.

Can you match a colour?

Yes. We mix every ink by hand, so send us a Pantone number or a swatch and we'll match it on the press.

Can you print my own artwork?

Yes. We make a photopolymer plate from your file (vector or 600 dpi), so logos and drawings print with the same bite as type.

Is letterpress more expensive?

For small runs, a little. For 250 business cards it's about the price of a good meal out, and people keep them.

Do you post?

Across the UK by tracked post, flat-packed between boards. Collect for free from the shop.
