01

Bring it to heat.

Mild steel bar goes into a coke fire at 1,250 °C. We read the temperature by colour, the way smiths always have: cherry, then orange, then straw.

1,250 °CFire
4 minTo forging heat
02

Draw it out.

Hammer blows on the anvil taper the end, about forty to a heat. When the colour drops below orange the bar goes back in the fire, never forced cold.

2 lbCross-pein
~40Blows a heat
03

Turn the scroll.

The tapered end is rolled over the horn, then closed on a scroll jig. Every scroll on a gate is matched by eye to its pair.

Finish and fit.

Dressed, wire-brushed and finished in beeswax and linseed, or hot-dip galvanised and painted for the street. Then we fit it ourselves.
